# Assessment example

## Objective

Given an AI-generated recommendation and source evidence, select the appropriate verification action.

## Scenario

An AI assistant recommends discontinuing Product B because “sales fell 30%.” The dashboard shows a 3% decline and notes incomplete data for the final month.

What should you do first?

A. Discontinue Product B before the decline worsens  
B. Replace 30% with 3% and publish the recommendation  
C. Verify the period and data completeness before recommending action  
D. Ask the AI assistant to make the recommendation sound less certain

**Correct answer:** C

## Feedback

- **A:** Not yet. The recommendation relies on an unsupported figure and incomplete data. Verify the evidence before taking action.
- **B:** Not yet. Correcting the percentage does not resolve the incomplete reporting period. Verify both the period and completeness.
- **C:** Correct. The evidence must be complete and comparable before it supports a business decision.
- **D:** Not yet. Softer wording does not fix weak evidence. Validate the underlying data.

## Confidence follow-up

How confident are you? **Low / Medium / High**

| Result | Route |
|---|---|
| Correct + High | Advance |
| Correct + Low/Medium | Show the verification checklist; advance |
| Incorrect + Low | Show a worked example; retry |
| Incorrect + High | Contrast the claim with the evidence; targeted retry |
