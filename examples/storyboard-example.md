# Storyboard example

**Topic:** Identifying unsupported AI claims  
**Audience:** Business analysts  
**Mode:** Lean

| Screen | Purpose | On-screen copy | Interaction | Feedback | Accessibility/developer notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish risk | **A confident answer can still be wrong.** Your task: verify claims before using them. | Continue | — | Heading announced first. No auto-advance. |
| 2 | Model the process | **Check three things:** source, evidence, and uncertainty. | Three-step process; each step opens one example | Each example identifies the relevant check | Buttons have descriptive labels; keyboard operable. |
| 3 | Guided practice | The AI states: “Revenue increased 18%.” The source table shows 8%. What should you do? | Choose: Accept / Correct / Escalate | Correct: “Correct the claim to 8% and cite the table.” Distractors explain the risk. | Do not use colour alone for feedback. |
| 4 | Independent decision | Review an AI summary against two supplied sources. Select every unsupported claim. | Multi-select | Targeted feedback by claim | Preserve source access during the task. |
| 5 | Transfer | **Before using AI output:** verify the source, match the evidence, note uncertainty. | Download job aid | — | Provide accessible PDF or HTML equivalent. |

## Objective

Given an AI-generated business summary and its sources, identify unsupported claims with 100% accuracy.

## Completion

Complete Screen 4 and review feedback. One retry allowed.
