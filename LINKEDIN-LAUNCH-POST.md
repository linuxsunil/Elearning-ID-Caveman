# LinkedIn launch post

Most AI-generated instructional-design content has one recurring problem:

It says too much before it teaches anything.

I created **E-learning ID Caveman**—an open-source AI skill for e-learning developers and instructional designers.

Its rule is simple:

**Say less. Teach more.**

It helps create concise:

- learning objectives;
- storyboards and learner scripts;
- assessments and targeted feedback;
- branching and confidence-based questions;
- Storyline and Rise specifications;
- accessibility and LMS QA reports.

But it will not shorten content at the cost of instructional alignment, accessibility, accuracy or technical detail.

The project is inspired by Julius Brussee’s Caveman concept and adapts the principle specifically for learning design.

GitHub: https://github.com/linuxsunil/Elearning-ID-Caveman

I would love feedback from instructional designers, e-learning developers and learning technologists. What should I add next?
